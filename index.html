Research

Undergraduate Thesis · DLSU · 2026

Legal Recognition of NFTs as Collateral in Philippine Secured Transactions

Business Regulatory and Compliance Risks, Challenges, and Reforms

The study looks at how non-fungible tokens (NFTs) are treated as collateral in the Philippines. It covers where NFTs fit in the current secured transactions framework, the regulatory and compliance risks for businesses that use them, and reforms that could give lenders and borrowers more legal certainty.
